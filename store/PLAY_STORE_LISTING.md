# BikersThrill — Google Play listing & every Play Console answer

Copy-paste answers for every Play Console form, in the order Console shows them.
Graphics are in `store/graphics/` and screenshots in `store/screenshots/`.

> **How search ranking really works.** The questionnaires (content rating, data safety and so on) don't raise
> your rank. They only have to be correct so the app is approved and shown to everyone. What ranks you is:
> the **title**, the **short description**, the **full description**, the **category**, a **Hindi listing**,
> and then **installs, retention and ratings**. All of these are covered below. Nobody can guarantee the #1 spot
> for broad words like "bike". The target is to be top for "bike group ride", "biker SOS", "motorcycle ride tracker"
> and similar phrases, which have far less competition.

## 1. Main store listing (Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing) — English (en-US / en-IN)

### App name (max 30) — 30/30
```
Bikers Thrill: Group Ride, SOS
```
Why **"Bikers Thrill"** has a space: Play matches whole words, so "BikersThrill" written as one word does **not**
match people who search "bikers" or "biker". With the space, the title matches *bikers*, *group ride* and *SOS*.
The phone's launcher label stays **BikersThrill**, and Play allows the two to differ.
Not allowed in the title (Play will reject the app): emoji, ALL CAPS, "free", "best", "#1", "top", or other brands.

## 6. Ranking plan — what to do, in order

1. **Before production:** use the title, short description, full description and Hindi listing above exactly as written.
   Changing the title later resets part of what Play has learned about the app, so get it right the first time.
2. **Launch-day burst.** Ask every closed tester and your riding clubs to install from the **Play Store link**
   (not an APK) in the first week. Install velocity in the first 7–14 days moves new apps most.
3. **Retention.** Riders who keep using the app and start rides tell Play the app is good. Plan a club ride in the
   app in launch week.
4. **Ratings and reviews.** Ask happy riders after a ride. Reviews that mention "group ride", "SOS" or
   "bikers" also help ranking for those words. Reply to every review.
   (An in-app review prompt after a finished ride can be added. Tell me if you want it.)
5. **Links from outside Play.** Put the Play link and a QR code on the website, Instagram/YouTube/Facebook
   bike-club pages and ride posters. Use a link like
   `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.satya.biker&referrer=utm_source=instagram`.
6. **Promo video.** Add a 30–60 s YouTube video of a group ride with the live map. It raises installs from the listing.
7. **Regular updates** with release notes, about every 2–4 weeks.
8. **After launch:** Play Console → Store listing experiments. A/B test the icon, first screenshot and short description.
9. **Keep crashes and ANRs low** (Android vitals). A bad-behaviour threshold lowers ranking.
