Delete your BikersThrill account

BikersThrill by Satya Digital (package com.satya.biker)

Option 1 – in the app (fastest)

  1. Open BikersThrill and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete account (or tap Delete account on the verification status screen).
  3. Confirm. If you signed in a while ago, the app asks you to sign in again first.

Option 2 – by email

If you can't use the app, email support.satyadigital@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject “Delete my BikersThrill account”. We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, phone, date of birth, gender, blood group, address, ID and licence numbers, bike details, emergency contacts, medical notes
  • Your ID card, driving licence and selfie photos
  • Your live location, SOS alerts and the rides you created; you are removed from your groups and rides
  • Your sign-in (email/password or Google)

What may be kept

Messages you posted in group chats stay visible to that group's members (shown with the name you used). Backups held by our cloud provider are overwritten within 30 days. We do not keep any other copy.
