BikersThrill

Live location sharing during a ride

How BikersThrill uses the FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION permission. Recorded with sample riders.

  1. The rider taps Start ride. The ride service starts and shares the rider's location with their own groups.
  2. Group members see the rider live on the group map.
  3. An ongoing notification is shown for the whole ride, with an End ride action.
  4. With the app in the background or the screen off, location sharing and crash detection keep running.
  5. The rider ends the ride from the notification or the app. The service stops and the notification is removed.
